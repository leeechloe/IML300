Koreantown

Some places you discover. Others you grow up inside of. BCD is the second kind. I can't remember the first time I went, it was just always there, a constant, the place my family would pile into after waiting what felt like hours, surrounded by family friends and the smell of soon dubu coming from every table. Rain or shine, it didn't matter. It was always the answer. Now I'm the one bringing people here. My college friends, when we want Korean food and nothing else will do. The tradition just quietly continued, different people around the table, same warmth in the bowl.

Anko Cafe

My friends and I stumbled into Anko by accident, just looking for somewhere to sit and study. In Koreatown, that's harder than it sounds, good seating, good drinks, and good wifi in the same place. Anko has all three! Now it's my go-to study spot, the kind of place you find and immediately want to keep a secret. The cloud latte is what I always get and honestly I think about it more than I should, and their red bean bingsoo hits on a hot sunny day. So so good!
