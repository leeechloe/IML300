Dear Los Angeles is a community archive of food memories across the city, where meals become stories tied to neighborhoods and people. Growing up in Los Angeles, I’ve come to know the city through the places I’ve eaten meals that hold comfort, routine, and memory. In a city this large and constantly shifting, food becomes one of the ways people create a sense of intimacy and belonging.
This project is important because it highlights how everyday experiences, like sharing a meal, can preserve personal and cultural memory in urban spaces that often feel fragmented or anonymous. It is created for people who live in, visit, or feel connected to Los Angeles. 
Love, Chloe Lee
