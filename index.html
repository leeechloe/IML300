Vibe Coding Experiments

Coming into IML 300, I had little to no experience when it comes to coding. I also had no clear understanding of what vibe coding even meant. Throughout the course, I enjoyed exploring vibe coding by experimenting with different prompts and ideas. It felt exciting to see how quickly something could be created without needing perfect technical knowledge. I appreciated how vibe coding encouraged creativity and reduced the pressure of making mistakes. It allowed me to focus more on expression rather than correctness. Overall, this experience helped me feel more confident, curious, and open to learning new ways of creating digital work.
